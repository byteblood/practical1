Quick Space Facts

Black holes

Regions of spacetime with gravity so strong that not even light can escape. They can form from collapsed stars.

Light-year

A measure of distance — about 9.46 trillion kilometres. It's how we measure distances to stars and galaxies.

Exoplanets

Planets orbiting other stars. Thousands have been discovered, and many are in the habitable zone.
